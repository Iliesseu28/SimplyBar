# Code of Conduct

SimplyBar follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, welcome people of every background and level of experience,
and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted, in issues, pull
requests or any other space of this project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, write to [contact@simplibot.fr](mailto:contact@simplibot.fr).
Every report is read and handled in confidence. The maintainers may remove comments, lock threads or block
people who break these rules.
